How to play

01

Read the clue

Each case hides one answer behind a single clue. The number of blocks shows how many letters it contains.

02

Submit a guess

Type with the on-screen or physical keyboard. Any combination of A–Z letters is accepted; every submitted guess uses one attempt.

03

Read the colours

Green means the letter and position are correct. Amber means the letter exists elsewhere. Red means it is not in the answer.

04

Use your aids

Two hint tokens are available each round. Reveal the first letter or unlock an extra clue. Each hint reduces your final score.

05

Beat the clock

You win when you decode the word before the timer or attempts run out. Solve quickly with unused guesses and no hints for the biggest score.

06

Build your record

Win consecutive cases to grow a streak, unlock badges, and keep your personal best. Rankings can be filtered by difficulty and daily cipher.

SCORE FORMULA base + (unused guesses × bonus) + (10s blocks left × rate) + no-hint bonus − (hints × penalty)

Unsuccessful cases score 0 completion points. Every hint is optional.
